About this demo

E-Shop Demo

Quality demo products for everyday life.

This is a generic portfolio demo store — not a real business. Products, prices and reviews are sample data you can fully manage from the admin panel.

What Is This?

E-Shop Demo is a portfolio project showing a complete e-commerce front-end built with vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript, backed by Supabase for products, orders, coupons and site settings.

Everything on this site is editable without touching code: products, categories, prices, stock, banners, coupons, payment instructions and theme colors are all managed from the admin panel.

What You Can Try

🛍️

Browse & Search

Filter by category, sort, search and open product pages with galleries and reviews.

🧾

Cart & Checkout

Add items to the cart, apply a coupon and place a demo order with bKash, Nagad, Rocket or Cash on Delivery instructions.

📦

Orders & Tracking

Sign in to see your orders, follow their status and track delivery on the tracking page.

⚙️

Admin Panel

Open the admin panel to manage products, stock, orders, coupons, banners and theme — no code changes needed.

Demo Notice

No real orders are fulfilled and no real payments are processed. Sample data (including product images) is for demonstration only.
